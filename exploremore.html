GOAL: Clean Water & Sanitation

Clean Water & Sanitation

Why the Goal Matters

The Goal aims to ensure clean water and sanitation for all. Water is essential for life, health, education, agriculture, and economic growth. Yet billions of people still struggle daily with water scarcity, pollution, and lack of proper sanitation.

Global Water Challenges

Every year, millions are affected by waterborne diseases. Communities without access to safe water and sanitation lose valuable time collecting water, miss school days, and face long-term health impacts. SDG 6 pushes for sustainable solutions that improve lives and protect ecosystems.

More Facts

Global Water Scarcity
Over 2 billion people live in countries experiencing high water stress.
Sanitation Crisis
3.6 billion people lack safely managed sanitation services.
Water Pollution
80% of wastewater flows back into rivers and oceans untreated.

Progress Timeline

2015: The planning of the goal.
2020: Countries strengthened monitoring on wastewater and hygiene.
2030 (Goal): Universal access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene.
